Tickets & Purchasing

Official FAQ
How can I book tickets?+

Choose an event on Open Tickets, select your ticket quantity and any required seating or category, then proceed through checkout. After payment, ticket access instructions are sent to the email used for the order.

Are tickets still available for an event?+

If a ticket category can be selected and added to your basket, it is available at that moment. Remaining quantities may not be published; availability can change before payment is completed.

Ticket Delivery & Downloading

Official FAQ
How do I transfer tickets?+

Use the Transfer Tickets option in your order email. Provide the recipient’s email address, choose how many tickets to transfer and follow the confirmation steps. The recipient must accept the transfer before its link expires.

I have not received my ticket email. What should I do?+

First check your inbox, spam, junk and promotions folders. If the message is still missing, contact Open Tickets support with the purchase name, email address, event and any available order or transaction reference.

How can I view or download my tickets?+

After a successful purchase, open your order email and use the available View Tickets, Apple Wallet, Google Wallet or supported download option. Some events, particularly football matches, use digital-only tickets.

Account & Profile

Official FAQ
How do I log in to My Account?+

Enter the same email address you used at checkout. Open Tickets will send a secure sign-in link to that email; follow the link to access the purchases associated with your account.

Do I need to create an account before buying?+

No. Open Tickets associates your purchase with the email used at checkout, so you can go directly to booking without registering a separate account first.

Why is my password not being accepted?+

Open Tickets uses an email sign-in link instead of a conventional password. Request a fresh link using the email address used for the booking and check your spam folder if it does not arrive.

Why can I not print my football tickets?+

Football tickets sold online through Open Tickets are issued digitally. Present the ticket on your device or in a supported mobile wallet; printed copies are not accepted for those matches.

General Information

Official FAQ
How do I view a ticket after purchase?+

Your order confirmation email includes the available ticket access options. Open it and choose the online view, mobile wallet or download method offered for that event. Multiple tickets may be accessed from the same email.

Where else can I buy event tickets?+

Tickets are available online. Certain events may also offer tickets through participating Shoprite, Checkers or Usave outlets. Check the individual event information because retail availability varies.

Is there an Open Tickets mobile app?+

Open Tickets does not currently provide a dedicated app. Your booking email contains options to view or save supported tickets to your phone.

Can I receive an alert when ticket sales open?+

Open Tickets does not currently operate an automatic on-sale notification feature. Check the official website and the event organiser’s announcements for release information.

Where can I see events happening this weekend?+

Browse the Events listing and check the event dates. You can also use the search and filter controls in this theme preview to narrow the local example listings.

Football & Match Day

Official FAQ
Does a child under six need a match ticket?+

Yes. Open Tickets states that each person attending its football matches requires a valid ticket, including young children. Always review the organiser’s rules for the specific match.

Where can I find my Orlando Pirates season card?+

Orlando Pirates season cards purchased through Open Tickets are digital. Follow the delivery and access instructions sent to your email; no physical card collection is required.

Which football matches are sold through Open Tickets?+

Open Tickets offers selected home matches for participating clubs and competitions. Not every fixture is available, so confirm the current schedule and ticket provider before travelling.
